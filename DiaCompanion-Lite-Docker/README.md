# DiaCompanion — Graduation Thesis 2026 (SE)

## Overview
This project is part of the **Graduation Thesis 2026 (Software Engineering)**.  
The research focuses on **diabetic retinopathy** and explores how **AI technology** can assist in large-scale screening and detection.

## Research Objectives
- **Study diabetic retinopathy** and its impact on patients with diabetes.  
- **Apply AI-based screening methods** using large international datasets.  
- **Validate results in the Vietnamese medical environment**, adapting models to local conditions.  
- **Develop a hospital-compatible system** that simulates deployment for doctors.  
- **Achieve consensus from medical professionals** on the feasibility and relevance of the proposed solution.

## Implementation Plan
1. **Dataset Analysis**: Utilize foreign datasets for initial AI model training.  
2. **Model Validation**: Re-test and fine-tune in Vietnamese clinical settings.  
3. **System Development**: Build a prototype compatible with existing hospital systems.  
4. **Doctor Simulation**: Provide a demo environment for physicians to test and evaluate.  
5. **Feedback & Consensus**: Gather opinions and agreement from doctors to validate the research direction.

## Expected Outcome
- A validated AI-based screening system for diabetic retinopathy.  
- A prototype hospital-compatible platform ready for simulated deployment.  
- Positive feedback and consensus from medical professionals in Vietnam.
